02 • Principals

03 • Teachers

AI classroom grading tools, lesson planning models, and student mental health.

04 • Admins/Ops

Branding frameworks, operational SOPs, budgeting, and tech integration.

Institutional Alignment

The Institutional Delegation

Separated for Depth. United for Execution.

But by attending under one delegation, the institutions return with unified insights across strategy, pedagogy, and operations.

2. Principal / Director PEDAGOGY & CULTURE

Dynamic Curation

Co-Created with Advisors

The TSOI Conclave does not run on a static, pre-packaged corporate agenda.

To ensure absolute relevance to the school year's actual challenges, the final schedule, panel debates, and scenario-planning labs will be designed dynamically in partnership with our Founding Board of Advisors, guaranteeing that every session directly addresses the most pressing problems in education today and work on solving them for the industry.
