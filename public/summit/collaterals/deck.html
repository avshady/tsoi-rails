Conclave Structure

Four Parallel Peer Circuits

To protect peer safety, leaders spend 80% of the summit in their isolated tracks, inclusive of stay & dining at Novotel, Imagicaa, Khopoli.

01 • Founders

Scaling frameworks, regulatory lobbying, JV capital strategy, and legal defense networks.

02 • Principals

Academic excellence systems, pedagogical leadership, and school culture design.

03 • Teachers

AI classroom grading tools, lesson planning models, and student mental health.

04 • Admins/Ops

Branding frameworks, operational SOPs, budgeting, and campus technology integration.
