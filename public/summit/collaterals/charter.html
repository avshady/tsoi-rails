1. Mandate & Purpose

The Schools of India Summit 2026 is the premier school leadership convergence. To ensure the curriculum remains academically rigorous, technologically current, and operationally practical, we are establishing the Founding Advisory Council.

The Council acts as the strategic brain trust of the summit. Comprising 20 to 30 distinguished school founders, trustees, and principals, the Council guides the thematic framework and invites peer leaders to secure their participation.

2. Council Composition

Council seats are allocated across educational disciplines to ensure a balanced, high-impact review board:

40% Founders

Trustees and owners focusing on institutional scale, branding, compliance, and growth.

30% Principals

Academic leaders focusing on school culture, teacher retention, and parent alignment.

30% Innovators

EdTech founders, authors, and policy representatives advising on trends.

3. Core Responsibilities

Advisors play an active role in building the conclave's footprint:

  • Curriculum Curation: Provide inputs on theme tracks, roundtable topics, and masterclass formats.
  • Speaker Nomination: Recommend and introduce global/domestic domain experts.
  • Session Leadership: Lead principal war rooms or moderate executive closed-door roundtables.
  • TSOI Ambassador: Promote the summit's mission to peer leaders across regional chapters.

4. Board Privileges

Founding Advisors are extended the following honorary privileges:

  • Complimentary passes: Two VIP All-Access delegate passes to the summit at Novotel Imagicaa Khopoli.
  • VIP Dinner: Private invitation to the Closed-Door Board Networking Dinner on Day 0.
  • Featured Recognition: Prominent portrait listing on digital portals, print brochures, and stage backdrops.
  • Pre-access: Early access to regional salary audits and administrative benchmarking reports.

5. Engagement Timeline

We respect your schedule and have structured a highly efficient roadmap:

July

Council Onboarding

Confirming seats and conducting a brief 1-on-1 alignment call with the convener.

August

Agenda Review

A single 60-minute virtual roundtable to review curriculum tracks and speaker suggestions.

September

Moderation Finalization

Confirming specific panel leader assignments and locking regional invitations.

Oct 30

Flagship Dinner & Summit

Convene at the Novotel Imagicaa for the Day 0 Closed-Door Dinner and lead sessions over the 3-day summit.
