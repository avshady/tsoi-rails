## 📝 Design & Print Layout Specifications

If you print this document as a booklet or export it as a PDF brochure, here are the typographic and styling guidelines:

1. **Cover Page**:
   * **Background**: Deep charcoal black (`#0a0a0c`) with a repeating vector SVG noise overlay at `3%` opacity.
   * **Visual Anchor**: Glowing neon violet/magenta linear logo emblem of a school crest integrated with a compass (representing leadership navigation).
   * **Border**: Thin 1px glow line wrapping the canvas with a `15px` margin.
   
2. **Body Layout**:
   * **Margins**: 2.5cm on all sides for clean print margins.
   * **Grid Layout**: 2-column tables for the "Conference Paradigm Shift (FROM ──> TO)" to ensure scannability.
   * **Accents**: Use vertical neon rules (`border-left: 3px solid #ff2a7f;`) to highlight key quotes, especially the "5% soul" quote.
   * **Day Timelines**: Arranged in horizontal step cards with rounded corners (`16px`) and subtle drop-glows.
