Dear Yash,

Education is undergoing an unprecedented structural shift—driven by artificial intelligence, evolving parent and community expectations, new operational realities, and rapidly changing pedagogical frameworks. These transformations demand thoughtful leadership, meaningful collaboration, and collective action rather than isolated responses.

In response, The Schools of India Summit 2026 is being established as a national platform that brings together school founders, trustees, principals, policymakers, innovators, and industry leaders to shape the future of education in India. More than a conference, the Summit is a collaborative movement dedicated to advancing educational leadership, institutional excellence, and transformative learning at scale.

To help shape this vision, we are inviting a select group of distinguished school founders, trustees, principals, and education leaders to join the Board of Advisors. As a Founding Advisor, your vision, experience, and leadership will help define the Summit’s strategic direction, guide its thematic focus, and contribute to a long-term roadmap for India’s K–12 and higher education ecosystem.

Summit Pillars

The summit's curriculum is organized across three progressive days of vision, system design, and institutional scale:

Vision

Anticipating shifts in technology, pedagogy, regulations, and institutional culture.

Systems

Translating strategy into solid operating procedures, assessments, and quality audits.

Growth

Optimizing admissions, school branding, scale, funding, and corporate partnerships.

Advisory Board Roles & Privileges

As a Founding Advisory Council member, you will have a direct hand in crafting the summit's footprint:

  • Summit Curation: Guide core tracks, session modules, and panel selections.
  • Speaker Nomination: Nominate and invite leading educational practitioners and global experts.
  • Speaking Leadership: Lead principal war rooms or moderate executive roundtables.

This is not an invitation to just attend the event. It is an invitation to help build an institution. We would be privileged to have your leadership help architect this milestone event. If this is a cause you want your name attached to from day one, we would love to hear from you.
