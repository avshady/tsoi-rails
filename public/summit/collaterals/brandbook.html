01 — Brand Story

A Summit Built for Those Who Build Schools.

The Schools of India Summit is the premier intellectual property that brings together the brightest minds in education—founders, directors, principals, vice principals, and EdTech innovators—for three days of bold strategy, collaboration, and pedagogical foresight.

The Strategic Need

Education is evolving at an unprecedented pace. The rise of generative AI, shifts in parent expectations, new national frameworks, and changing school models require leaders to continuously learn. The summit exists to bridges the gap between vision and operational tools.

Who We Serve

We serve the decision-makers. The founders who allocate capital, the directors who set the vision, the principals who build the community, and the teachers who design classroom experiences. We give them space to network and align.

Brand Architecture

Atmospheric, premium, block-based, and highly energetic. By steering clear of child-like primary colors, the brand positions itself as a luxury professional conference. It takes inspiration from elite design and creator forums.

The Curation Promise

We do not run generalized, flat talks. Every session, masterclass, and roundtable is led by verified domain experts, providing school leaders with structured frameworks, toolkits, and launch-ready tactics.

Our Core Pillars

Vision — Anticipating shifts in technology, pedagogy, and community expectations.

Collaboration — Creating space for unscripted, peer-to-peer dialogues at roundtables and dinners.

Action — Equipping delegates with toolkits and blueprints they can deploy on Day 1.

06 — Light & Dark Mode

Two modes, one visual system.

The brand is dark-first by default to project premium tech authority. Light mode is provided to accommodate accessibility needs. Note that contrast ratios shift; never use dark-mode neon colors on light canvas fields.

07 — Buttons & Badges

Interactive UI components.

Pill geometries govern all interactive buttons. Category badges use low-opacity tinted fills to differentiate educational themes visually.

Button variants
  • Primary Button: Solid white in dark mode (solid dark in light mode). Reserved for core conversion triggers (Register, Confirm).
  • Secondary Button: Semi-transparent backdrop with active border highlights. Used for secondary discovery paths (Become a Partner, View Sponsors).
  • Padding: 9px 24px. Typography: Outfit 600, 13px size. Pill shape (radius: 100px).
AI in Education Future Schools Growth & Branding
Thematic category badges
  • Used to tag summit agenda items, card themes, and exhibition areas.
  • Utilizes a tinted backdrop (5% opacity of accent) with a matching solid border (20% opacity) and high-contrast text.
  • Keeps the layout organized and color-coded without cluttering the screen.

09 — Motion Curves

Transitions and micro-animations.

Motion should feel fluid, intentional, and high-end. Transitions avoid linear mechanics and utilize custom cubic-bezier dampening.

Smooth Entry / Transform

Applied to sliding drawers (CMS Editor), page transitions, and card expansions.

transition: all 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Hover / Active States

Applied to button color shifts, tab clicks, and interactive link underlines.

transition: all 0.2s ease-in-out
